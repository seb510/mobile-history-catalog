Sony Ericsson was established in October 2001 as a joint venture between Japan’s Sony and Sweden’s Ericsson. Sony contributed consumer-electronics and multimedia experience, while Ericsson brought telecommunications engineering; the headquarters were in London.

Sliding the lens cover open on the [K750i](/en/phones/sony-ericsson-k750i/) in 2005 launched the camera. Held sideways, it worked like a compact camera: press the side shutter button halfway to focus, then all the way to take the picture. That same year, the orange-and-white [W800i](/en/phones/sony-ericsson-w800i/) gave music its own button. It opened the Walkman player, with tracks stored on a Memory Stick Duo card.

In 2008, the [Xperia X1](/en/phones/sony-ericsson-xperia-x1/) made room for longer messages. Sliding the screen sideways revealed a full keyboard for typing an email in Windows Mobile with both thumbs.

The Android-powered Xperia X10 followed in 2010. In 2012, Sony bought Ericsson’s share and the division continued as Sony Mobile.
