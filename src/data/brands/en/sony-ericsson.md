Sony Ericsson was established in October 2001 as a joint venture between Japan’s Sony and Sweden’s Ericsson. Sony contributed consumer-electronics and multimedia experience, while Ericsson brought telecommunications engineering; the headquarters were in London.

The Cyber-shot and Walkman series gave the brand a strong identity in photography and music, with phones such as the K750i, W800i and K800i. In 2012, Sony bought Ericsson’s share, and the mobile division continued as Sony Mobile.
