Nokia began in Finland in 1865 with paper manufacturing. Through cables, rubber and electronics, the company moved into telecommunications and focused on mobile communications in the 1990s.

The 3210, 3310 and 1100 became symbols of the mass-market keypad era. Nokia also developed Symbian smartphones and unusual Nseries designs. In 2014, its phone business was sold to Microsoft; phones carrying the Nokia name later returned through HMD Global.
