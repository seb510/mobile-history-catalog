The Alcatel name dates back to 1898, within the French telecommunications group Compagnie Générale d'Électricité. The company only built its own mobile phone division in the 1990s, launching the One Touch brand in 1998.

In 2004 the mobile phone business became a joint venture between Alcatel and China's TCL, which took full control a year later. In our archive, Alcatel is represented by the most affordable tier of the One Touch line — phones built around price rather than features.
