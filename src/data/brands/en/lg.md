LG was founded in 1958 in South Korea as GoldStar, a company that made the country's first radios, televisions and refrigerators. In 1995, after merging with Lucky Chemical, the electronics division took the name LG Electronics.

In mobile phones, LG was known for practical mass-market models — from budget clamshells in the early 2000s to later experiments with displays and processors. The company exited the smartphone market entirely in 2021.
