Siemens is a German industrial company founded in 1847, long before mobile communications. Its phone division grew out of its telecommunications business. Models such as the C55, S55 and SL55 made the brand particularly recognisable during the early-2000s keypad era.

In 2005, the mobile business was sold to Taiwan’s BenQ and continued under the BenQ-Siemens name. In this archive, Siemens stands for distinctive keypads, light signals, interchangeable covers and experiments with sound: features that made a phone recognisable even without its logo.
