HTC was founded in Taiwan in 1997 as a contract manufacturer. It made handheld computers and smartphones for other brands and network operators before becoming widely recognised under its own name in the mid-2000s.

In 2008, HTC released the Dream, also known as the T-Mobile G1, the first commercial Android phone. Its history brings together hardware keyboards, early touch interfaces and the search for a distinctive smartphone design.
