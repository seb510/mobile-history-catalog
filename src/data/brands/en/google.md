Larry Page and Sergey Brin founded Google in 1998. The company grew from a search engine into a developer of services, Android, and its own devices.

The Pixel phone line began in 2016. Software features, computational photography, and Google service integration define its approach. Pixel 6 introduced Tensor, while Pixel 8 brought a seven-year update commitment.

Source: [Google — Our story](https://about.google/company-info/our-story/), [Pixel і Tensor](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-tensor-debuts-new-pixel-6-fall/), [Pixel 8](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/software-support-pixel-8-pixel-8-pro/).

Pixel 9 refreshed the series design in 2024 with flat sides and a separate oval camera bar.
