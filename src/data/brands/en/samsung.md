Samsung was founded in South Korea in 1938 as a trading company. Electronics and mobile phones became part of its business much later.

In the early 2000s, the brand became known for clamshells and sliders, including the SGH-E700 and SGH-E250. The Galaxy series later underpinned its move into Android smartphones. In the archive, Samsung connects keypad mechanisms with more recent experiments in folding screens.
