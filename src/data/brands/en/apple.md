Apple was founded in the United States in 1976 as a personal-computer company. It entered the mobile-phone market much later than Nokia, Motorola and other established manufacturers.

In January 2007, Apple introduced the first iPhone. Its large touchscreen and multitouch interface replaced a physical keypad and helped define the modern smartphone. In this archive, the iPhone illustrates a change in how people interacted with a phone.
