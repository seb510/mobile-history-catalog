Huawei is a Chinese technology company whose smartphones became known through the Ascend and Mate series. Mobile photography and its own software platforms have played a prominent role in the development of its phones.

The Mate XT continued its experiments with form: instead of a single fold, it uses two hinges and three screen sections. In the archive, it continues the story of phone construction beyond the keypad era.
