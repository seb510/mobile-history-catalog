Motorola was founded in the United States in 1928. Its history connects car radios with the development of portable communications, including the DynaTAC cellular phone.

During the keypad era, it became known for V-series clamshells and the 2004 RAZR V3. The mobile business later passed to Google in 2012 and Lenovo in 2014. The archive focuses on the metal RAZR, rounded PEBL and musical ROKR experiments.
