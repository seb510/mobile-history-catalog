The P800’s flip keys operate over its touchscreen. Folding back or removing the panel changes the interaction from calls to stylus and organiser.

UIQ, Jog Dial and Memory Stick Duo define this early 2002 smartphone. The pictured phone has its flip removed, an intended mode rather than a missing body part.
