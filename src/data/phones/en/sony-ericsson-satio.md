Sony Ericsson showed this handset as the Idou at Mobile World Congress in 2009. The production Satio had a wide resistive display. Turn it sideways and the dedicated shutter button fell under your finger, with a xenon flash for pictures in low light.

Its touch menus ran on Symbian with S60 5th Edition. For someone coming from an earlier Sony Ericsson P-series smartphone, that meant moving from UIQ to a different application environment.
