Siemens announced the S10 in March 1997. Its 97 × 54 display showed red, green and blue on a white background. The screen held up to six lines of text, with fewer lines when the user chose the larger type size.

A dedicated side button recorded a voice memo of up to 20 seconds. The external antenna and numeric keypad kept the familiar shape of a late-1990s GSM handset.
