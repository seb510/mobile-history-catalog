The ME75 was introduced in October 2005 as a smaller, lighter alternative to the rugged Siemens M75 — around 85% of its weight, with similar resistance to dust, shock and splashes. A rubberised rim around the body and thickened corners kept it looking like an ordinary candybar rather than an overtly "outdoor" phone.

A swivel cover protected the rear VGA camera: closed, it shielded the lens from scratches and dust; opened, it woke the camera for shooting. The ME75's announcement landed right as Siemens' mobile division moved into the BenQ-Siemens joint venture in autumn 2005.
