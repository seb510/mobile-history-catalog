With the W705 closed, the Walkman key opened the player and the navigation pad let you skip a track. Sliding the screen up revealed the number pad for calls and messages.

Sony Ericsson announced the phone at the MTV Europe Music Awards in Liverpool in November 2008. Alongside a music library on a memory card, Wi-Fi gave the slider a way onto a home network.
