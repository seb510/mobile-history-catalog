In 2009, Nokia introduced the 6700 classic as a successor to the 6300. Its stainless-steel case and metal keypad kept the familiar shape, while a dedicated button opened the 5-megapixel autofocus camera.

Series 40 kept calls and messages familiar. With 3G and A-GPS, Nokia Maps made downloading maps and finding your way possible on a conventional keypad phone.
