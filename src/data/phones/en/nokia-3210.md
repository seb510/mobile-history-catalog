The 1999 Nokia 3210 represents a moment when mobile phones became personal belongings far beyond business communication. Its internal antenna and interchangeable front and rear Xpress-on covers made it easy to carry and change its appearance without replacing the handset.

- **Make it your own.** Personalisation went beyond colour. Composer let users create ringtones, while predefined picture messages could accompany SMS. Calls and greetings acquired an individual character long before smartphone app stores and social networks.
- **Everyday messages and games.** Predictive text used one keypress per letter instead of repeatedly cycling through a key's characters. Games including Snake offered another role: a brief diversion always close at hand.

The 3210 matters here for this combination of compact communication, messages and personal expression. Comparing it with the following Nokia 3310 traces the development of a mass-market GSM phone while retaining the basic button-operated logic.
