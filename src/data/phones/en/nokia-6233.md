The Nokia 6233 arrived in 2005 as a candybar that brought 3G into a familiar keypad design. Its 2-inch QVGA display, 2 MP camera and microSD slot sat alongside the conventional number pad.

Music was part of the hardware, not just the menus: the body has two stereo speakers. The phone also includes FM radio, Bluetooth, infrared and WCDMA on the 2100 MHz band. A removable BP-6M battery and memory card kept key parts accessible without moving to a smartphone platform.

In this catalogue, the 6233 makes an interesting comparison with the Nokia N70 from the same year. Both combine 3G with a 2 MP camera, but the N70 runs Symbian and has a sliding camera cover, while the 6233 keeps the Series 40 format and puts two speakers on its body.
