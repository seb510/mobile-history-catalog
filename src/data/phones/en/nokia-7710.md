The Nokia 7710 belongs to an early branch of touchscreen smartphones. Its 3.5-inch, 640 × 320 wide display and stylus input were designed for web pages, documents and video—not today’s familiar portrait interface.

It was the only commercial model to use Symbian’s Series 90 interface. The supplied 128 MB MMC card was separate media; up to 90 MB refers to available internal storage, not RAM or card capacity. Nokia’s external DVB-H Streamer SU-22 was used in trials, rather than sold as a standard consumer accessory.
