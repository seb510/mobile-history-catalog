The Nokia 7280 from the 2004 Fashion Collection proposed a different gesture: turn a spinner and select characters on screen instead of pressing number keys. Its narrow body, mirror details and black, white and red palette presented the phone as a fashion accessory.

A short movement of the active slide answered or ended calls. Entering names and messages through a character strip demanded a different habit from ordinary T9 typing. That trade-off between appearance and everyday input gives the 7280 its place in this archive.

Behind the unusual shape were a VGA camera, Bluetooth, infrared and FM radio. Its battery was internal; Nokia’s guide specified replacement by a service facility. This is the 7280, distinct from the later 7380 fashion model.
