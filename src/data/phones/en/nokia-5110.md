Nokia introduced the 5110 in March 1998 as a mass-market model and began volume shipments in May. Xpress-on covers let owners change its colour, while the external antenna and physical keypad defined its outline.

The central Nokia Navi key changed function according to the on-screen prompt. The menus included text messaging and three games, including Snake.
