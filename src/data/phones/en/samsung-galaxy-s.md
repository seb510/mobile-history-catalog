The first Galaxy S began Samsung's flagship Android line in 2010. Super AMOLED was central to its identity, alongside a slim body and the Hummingbird processor later known as Exynos 3110.

- **The display takes centre stage.** Super AMOLED made the screen a defining part of the device's presentation, rather than simply another component.
- **Capture and communicate.** A 5 MP camera recorded HD video and a front VGA camera supported video calls. Internet access, photography and communication increasingly shared one device.

Samsung's retrospective reports more than 25 million Galaxy S phones sold worldwide as of 2013. This is a dated manufacturer figure, not a final lifetime total. The gallery's CyanogenMod 11 photograph shows community software, not the original factory system.
