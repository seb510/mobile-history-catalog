Pixel 9 introduced a new design language for the 2024 generation: flat front and back surfaces, rounded corners, and a separate oval camera bar in place of the band that joined the frame. The familiar horizontal layout remained, now expressed as a distinct element.

The standard Pixel 9 brought a 6.3-inch Actua display, Tensor G4, and 12 GB of RAM. It launched alongside Pixel 9 Pro and Pixel 9 Pro XL, offering the Pro in two sizes for the first time: 6.3 and 6.8 inches. The standard model represents this series-wide design change in the timeline.

Sources: [Google — Pixel 9 design](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-9-series-design/), [Google — Pixel 9, Pro and Pro XL](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/devices/pixel/google-pixel-9-pro-xl/).
