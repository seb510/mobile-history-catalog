The K610i brought 3G and two cameras to a familiar candybar handset with a numeric keypad. Its front camera handled UMTS video calls, and a dedicated key launched the main camera. A Memory Stick Micro card provided extra space for photos and music.

Ericsson’s annual report named the K610 family among Sony Ericsson’s best-selling WCDMA phones in 2006. It illustrates how video calling was reaching compact everyday handsets.
