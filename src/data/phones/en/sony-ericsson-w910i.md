The W910i made movement part of listening. A thin sliding front conceals the number keys, while Walkman 3.0 brings music to the foreground. Shake control changes tracks with a gesture; SensMe groups music by mood and tempo.

- A dedicated Walkman key returns quickly to the player.
- M2 cards supplement the small internal file storage.
- The media interface supports portrait and landscape viewing.

This 2007 slider remained prominent the following season: the W910 won Best Mobile Handset or Device at the 2008 Global Mobile Awards. Its appeal lies in the relationship between music, movement and a slim sliding body.
