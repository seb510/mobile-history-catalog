The SGH-U600 belonged to Ultra Edition II, where thickness became part of its name: Ultra Edition 10.9. In April 2007, Samsung announced the European rollout beginning in France. Thinness was the central design idea rather than an isolated specification.

- **A continuous surface.** Samsung emphasised a smooth exterior without visible screws or small recesses, a mirror-like front and gemstone-inspired colours.
- **Metal details.** Samsung's later materials history identifies metal elements in the U600. This does not mean the entire body was solid metal.

The U600 represents the mature slider era, when an opening mechanism coexisted with a drive towards a slim silhouette. [Compare it with the D500](/en/compare/?a=samsung-sgh-d500&b=samsung-sgh-u600): within a few years, emphasis shifted towards thickness, surfaces and concealed mechanics.
