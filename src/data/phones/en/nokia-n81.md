Nokia unveiled the N81 on 29 August 2007 alongside the relaunch of the N-Gage gaming platform — together with the N73, N93 and N95, it was part of the first wave of compatible S60 devices. The phone reached retail on 22 October 2007 in two versions at once: a standard model with a microSD slot, and the N81 8GB with built-in storage instead of a card.

The slider hid a numeric keypad, with a navigation wheel above it flanked by two hotkeys for gaming and media, plus a mechanical lock switch on top of the casing. According to the technical documentation, the N81 was the first Nseries device to use a micro-USB port instead of the larger Pop-Port.

At launch, N-Gage 2.0 was available only as a public beta — the N81 was sold as an "N-Gage ready" device preloaded with demo versions of FIFA 07, Asphalt 3: Street Rules and Space Impact Light, rather than a phone with a complete gaming service from day one. The 2-megapixel camera was deliberately a secondary feature: the model's priorities were stereo speakers, dedicated music keys and the gaming experience, not photography.

The N81 has no built-in GPS, but it does have FM / Visual Radio, using a compatible wired headset as its antenna. Maps can use a compatible external Bluetooth GPS receiver. Bluetooth 2.0 with A2DP and Wi-Fi 802.11b/g complement its music features.

### N81 and N81 8GB

Nokia offered both versions: the standard N81 stores files on microSD, while the N81 8GB uses a separate nominal 8 GB mass-storage drive instead of a card slot. This is not 8 GB of RAM. Available space is lower than nominal capacity and depends on stored content. The differences appear in the Storage and Memory card rows of the same technical portrait.
