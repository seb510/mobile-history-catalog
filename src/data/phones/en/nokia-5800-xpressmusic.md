On 2 October 2008, Nokia introduced the 5800 XpressMusic as a mass-market music phone. Its touchscreen joined familiar Nokia features: stereo speakers, a Carl Zeiss camera and expandable memory. It was a key step towards touch-operated S60, rather than Nokia's first touchscreen device of any kind.

- **Several ways to touch.** The resistive screen accepted a finger, stylus or the included plectrum. Media Bar opened music, photographs, video and the internet; Contacts Bar gathered four important people with their messages and events.
- **A personal music library.** A standard 3.5 mm jack, stereo sound and an included 8 GB card supported the music identity. The €279 estimate before taxes and subsidies applied to the ordinary model. A separate Comes With Music version was planned later, so its catalogue access should not be assumed for every 5800.

The model offered button-phone Nokia users a more accessible route to touch smartphones. Compared with the original iPhone, it shows different approaches to music, text input and personal accessories as well as screen technology.
