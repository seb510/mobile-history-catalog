The N93 unfolds and twists its screen into a landscape viewfinder. Dedicated shutter and zoom controls support a camcorder-style grip.

Its 3× optical zoom and stereo VGA recording distinguish it from a conventional flip phone. miniSD storage and TV output continue the workflow after filming.

[Manufacturer document](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2006/04/25/1847675/0/en/Nokia-Introduces-the-Next-Story-in-Video-with-the-Nokia-N93.html).

## N93i — the same gesture, a different face

Nokia announced the N93i on 8 January 2007. A mirrored cover and metallic keypad changed its appearance while retaining the recognisable twisting screen and camcorder grip.

- **Body.** At 163 g, the N93i is lighter than the 180 g N93; its dimensions are 108 × 58 × 25 mm.
- **Display.** The diagonal remains 2.4 inches, with up to 16 million colours.
- **Recording.** It retains the 3.2 MP camera, 3× optical zoom and VGA recording at up to 30 fps with stereo audio. The standard N93i sales pack included a 1 GB miniSD card.
