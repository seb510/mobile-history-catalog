The 1998 Motorola cd930 was a candybar phone with an exposed keypad and support for both 900 and 1800 MHz GSM networks. The Smart key and volume buttons made it quick to look up a phone-book entry.

A dedicated button started the VoiceNotes recorder. The phone could hold up to three minutes of recordings, enough to dictate an address or a reminder on the move.
