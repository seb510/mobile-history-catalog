The Samsung Soul paired a slider with a 5 MP camera and Magical Touch: its touch-sensitive navigation panel changed icons to match the active feature. An ordinary navigation area could take on several contextual roles.

The SGH-U900 was introduced in 2008 as part of Ultra Edition. These specifications describe the standard global model; network support varied in regional variants with suffixes.
