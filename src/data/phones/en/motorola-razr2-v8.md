RAZR2 was introduced on 15 May 2007, with availability in selected countries planned for July. Motorola developed the clamshell silhouette through a metal internal frame, glass outer surface and larger external display.

- **Use it while closed.** The 2-inch outer display exposed some functions without opening the lid. A later US announcement described touch-sensitive external keys with haptic feedback: an evolution of the closed clamshell, rather than a full touchscreen smartphone.
- **One family, different networks.** V8 used GSM, V9 added HSDPA and V9m used CDMA/EV-DO. The family's 3G features should not be assigned to the V8. Motorola also described revised contacts, main-menu customisation and spoken interface elements on V8.
