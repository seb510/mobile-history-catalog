The KRZR K1 carried the RAZR design into a narrower, glossy body. The clamshell kept a slim profile but gained its own proportions and finish; visual character mattered as much as the feature set.

This entry covers the GSM K1, not the CDMA K1m. The K1 had a 2 MP camera and microSD expansion; regional and carrier versions could differ.
