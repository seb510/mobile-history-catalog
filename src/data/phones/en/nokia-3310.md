The Nokia 3310 succeeded the 3210 and became one of the most recognizable button phones. Xpress-on covers, SMS, and Snake II made it an icon of mass-market mobile communication in the early 2000s.

This is the original Nokia 3310 (NHM-5, 2000), not the 2017 HMD revival. Its screen is 84 × 48 pixels. It has no cameras or memory-card slot. SIM phonebook capacity is not RAM; verified numerical RAM and user flash capacities are not listed here. The service documentation identifies MAD2; its 13 MHz radio clock is not treated as a confirmed CPU clock.
