The 2003 Siemens M55 made a phone personal through light, sound and an unusual keypad. Orange keys form an X-shaped pattern across its silver face.

- **Light.** Dynamic Light indicators on the sides flash in different rhythms for calls, messages and alarms. The menu includes a light-pattern demonstration.
- **Music.** Steinberg’s Cubasis mobile let owners create their own melodies, turning the phone into a small tool for sound experiments.
- **Photography as an accessory.** There is no built-in camera. The separate QuickPic IQP-500 attaches to the phone and takes VGA photographs with a flash.
