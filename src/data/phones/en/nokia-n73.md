Nokia unveiled the N73 in April 2006. Its sliding rear cover protected the lens and activated the camera when opened. A dedicated shutter button and autofocus made taking photos feel closer to using a compact camera.

Compared with the N70, it had a larger 2.4-inch display and moved to S60 3rd Edition. You could browse photos on the phone, send them or transfer them to a computer. Nokia also announced an N73 Music Edition that autumn.
