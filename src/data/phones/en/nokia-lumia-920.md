The Lumia 920 kept the colourful body of early Lumia phones and was among Nokia’s first Windows Phone 8 handsets. Resizable Start tiles let users decide how much room to give each app.

PureView optical stabilisation helped in low light. Built-in Qi charging let the phone rest on a charging pad without plugging a cable into the handset.
