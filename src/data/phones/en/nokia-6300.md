The Nokia 6300 paired a familiar keypad candybar with a slim body and metal details. It offered a restrained alternative to phones defined by bright colours or unusual mechanisms.

Its QVGA display, 2 MP camera and microSD slot made the 6300 a versatile everyday handset. Announced late in 2006, it is listed here as a 2007 phone.
