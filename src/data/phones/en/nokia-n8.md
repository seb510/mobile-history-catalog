N8 put photography at the heart of Nokia’s flagship: a large phone camera sensor, Carl Zeiss optics, Xenon flash and a dedicated shutter key. HDMI brought photos and videos to a TV, while USB On-The-Go connected peripherals.

It marked Nokia’s move to Symbian^3, followed later by Anna and Belle updates. The table shows the launch OS. Its 16 GB mass memory is separate from the 512 MB ROM. The battery can be replaced after disassembly, but is not designed for routine user removal.
