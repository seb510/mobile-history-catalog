Apple began selling the iPhone 17 in September 2025. The base model gained a 6.3-inch OLED display with up to 120 Hz ProMotion and started at 256 GB. The higher refresh rate made scrolling and interface animations smoother.

The Center Stage front camera could take landscape group selfies while the phone was held upright. Counterpoint Research ranked the base iPhone 17 among the top ten smartphones by global sales in 2025.
