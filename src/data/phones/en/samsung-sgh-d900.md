The SGH-D900 was a slim slider with a 3.15 MP autofocus camera. Metal trim and a shallow body gave the familiar mechanism a more polished, almost jewellery-like presence.

This is the D900, not the later D900i; radio and network features should not be carried over from that revision. The model was part of Samsung’s slim Ultra Edition line in 2006.
