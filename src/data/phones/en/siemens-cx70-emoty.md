The CX70 Emoty was a variant of the standard CX70 with a feature unique for 2004: stroke, press and shake sensors built into the sides of the case controlled animated 3D characters — Laura, Joey and Wobble. A gesture became an "emotional" animation that could be sent as an MMS to any MMS-capable phone — instead of choosing words, the user simply "showed" a mood through motion.

Under the hood it was the same CX70: a TFT display at 132 × 176 pixels and 65,000 colors, a built-in VGA camera with video recording, and 9.5 MB of internal storage with no card slot. Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and radio were absent — only an infrared port for data exchange.

It was unveiled in Q3 2004, going on sale in Europe and the Asia-Pacific region from Q4 that year. This was a marketing experiment in emotional interface design for Siemens, not a technical successor to the CX70.
