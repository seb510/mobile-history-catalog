Introduced in June 2008, the Omnia i900 combined Windows Mobile 6.1 with Samsung's own widget interface, drag-and-drop interaction and haptic feedback. It represents a path towards touch smartphones before the flagship Galaxy S series.

- **Work on the screen.** Samsung highlighted office documents, push email, multitasking and Opera 9.5. An optical navigation control offered another input method, resembling a small laptop touchpad.
- **Multimedia alongside work.** A 5 MP camera, GPS, Wi-Fi and internal storage complemented the productivity features. The removable battery was documented in the manual's cover-removal and installation instructions.

The international SGH-i900 should not be conflated with Korean T Omnia variants, which had different specifications. [Compare it with the Galaxy S](/en/compare/?a=samsung-omnia-i900&b=samsung-galaxy-s) to explore Samsung's platform change between 2008 and 2010.
