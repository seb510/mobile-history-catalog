The 8600 Luna turns the brief movement of a slider into part of the phone’s character. Nokia paired stainless steel with dark, almost opaque glass, concealing the keypad underneath.

The backlight softly pulsed through the glass, while the Luna name evoked moonlight. Nokia also described it as its first phone with a micro-USB port, combining charging, audio and data. Its launch price was about €700.
