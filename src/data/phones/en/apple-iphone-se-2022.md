The third-generation iPhone SE went on sale in March 2022. It paired the A15 Bionic chip and 5G with a compact 4.7-inch display and a Home button with Touch ID. Owners could upgrade while keeping a familiar way to unlock and navigate the phone.

Counterpoint Research placed the 2022 iPhone SE among the ten best-selling smartphones worldwide that year.
