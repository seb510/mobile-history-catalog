Nokia announced the 600 in August 2011 as a music-focused Symbian Belle smartphone. The company highlighted its loud built-in speaker, FM radio with an integrated antenna and an FM transmitter for playing music through a conventional radio. The announced feature set also included NFC, a 1 GHz processor and a 5-megapixel camera with LED flash.

The Nokia 600 should be described as an announced device: it never reached retail, as its launch was cancelled. The archive therefore uses placeholder artwork, and its specifications describe the planned phone.
