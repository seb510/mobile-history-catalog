The circular keypad makes the 3650 recognisable before it powers on. Series 60, a VGA camera and replaceable Xpress-on covers combined experimentation with everyday use.

Announced in September 2002, it began shipping in 2003, the year used here. Nokia specifies 3.4 MB of user memory, rather than a rounded 4 MB.
