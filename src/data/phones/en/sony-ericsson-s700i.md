The S700i rotates its upper section through 180 degrees to uncover the number keys. Closed, it still leaves the screen and navigation controls accessible. Turn it over and the arrangement resembles a compact camera, with a lens shutter that activates shooting.

The S700 family arrived in 2004 with a 1.3MP CCD camera, QVGA screen and Memory Stick Duo. Bluetooth and infrared provided ways to move photographs. Its music player predates Walkman branding. These photographs show the regional S700c and are labelled accordingly; the specifications describe the S700i.
