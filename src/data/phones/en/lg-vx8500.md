The US LG VX8500 carried the Chocolate name but differed from the international KG800 slider: it was a clamshell with touch-sensitive media controls on its face. A glossy black body brought keypad use together with gesture-like music controls.

The model used CDMA networks. Do not transfer KG800 or another regional Chocolate’s specifications to this phone simply because the names are related.
