The US LG VX8500 Chocolate is a slider with touch-sensitive media controls on its face. Its glossy body conceals a sliding numeric keypad; it is a Verizon CDMA model, not the international GSM KG800.

The model used CDMA networks. Do not transfer KG800 or another regional Chocolate’s specifications to this phone simply because the names are related.
