The LG C1300 (also known under the internal designation G4015) continued LG's most affordable clamshell line — a direct successor to the 4010/4011 models. The main upgrade was a colour CSTN display at 128 × 128 pixels, replacing the predecessors' monochrome screen, in a smaller body.

The phone targeted the US market: it received FCC certification on 27 February 2004 and was sold through AT&T Wireless, Cellular One and Dobson Communications. Java app support, MMS/EMS messaging and a T9 dictionary were the standard set for a budget GSM phone of the mid-2000s; the model has no camera and no memory card support.
