The Sony Ericsson K550i made the camera part of everyday interaction: an active lens cover revealed a 2 MP autofocus module with an LED photo light. The candybar had a physical camera gesture of its own.

This is the 2007 Cyber-shot model, not the music-focused W610i from the same era. The specifications describe the GSM K550i; regional K550 variants differed.
