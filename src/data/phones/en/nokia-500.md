In 2011, the Nokia 500 brought touchscreen smartphone features to a more affordable tier. Its plain candybar body came with interchangeable coloured back covers and Symbian Anna. A single-core 1 GHz ARM11 processor powered the 3.2-inch, 640 × 360 display.

The 5-megapixel fixed-focus camera had no flash. Music, photos and other files could be kept in internal storage or expanded with a microSD card; the removable BL-4U battery could be swapped without a service visit.
