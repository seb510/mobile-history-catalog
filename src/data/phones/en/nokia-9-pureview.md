Nokia 9 PureView makes its camera array the defining feature of the back. Five cameras capture together rather than providing five focal lengths, combining their data into a 12-megapixel image with RAW DNG support.

Announced at MWC 2019, this is an HMD-era Nokia flagship, not a device from the original Nokia handset division. It closes the flagship branch of this selection; later budget, rugged and feature phones belong to different ranges.
