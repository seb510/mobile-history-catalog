The 2003 N-Gage attempted to combine mobile communication and dedicated games. Nokia called it a game deck and planned a global launch for 7 October. Beforehand, a European tour covering more than 50 cities offered hands-on play: the launch centred on a gaming community as well as phone specifications.

- **Play nearby or connect.** Bluetooth enabled local multiplayer, while GPRS brought network features. This was a dedicated gaming scenario built around physical controls and specific game titles, rather than today's smartphone games.
- **Several devices in one pocket.** An MP3 player and stereo FM radio joined the phone and portable-console roles. Combining them demanded a new balance between gaming controls and conventional phone operation.

The N-Gage remains an early experiment in mobile gaming platforms before modern smartphone app stores. This page covers the original 2003 device; the later N-Gage QD and software service sharing the name are separate stages.

Source: [Nokia — N-Gage Tour and global launch date, 21 July 2003](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2003/07/21/1846957/0/en/nokia-n-gage-tm-tour-announced.html).
