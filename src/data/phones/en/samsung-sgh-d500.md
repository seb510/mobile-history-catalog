The SGH-D500 paired a slider body with a dark blue-black finish. Samsung includes it in its design history as a 2004 model that stood out among the light-colored phones then in circulation. Sliding the screen revealed the numeric keypad: the body's mechanism became part of everyday interaction.

In February 2005, the D500 won the best mobile phone award at the 3GSM World Congress in Cannes. That confirms the model's recognition at the time, but does not mean every specification was best in class or that other manufacturers copied this phone specifically.

In August 2006, Samsung reported that D500 sales had passed 10 million, separately from the successor D600's figure. In the timeline, it illustrates the combination of mechanical form and a distinctive finish.
