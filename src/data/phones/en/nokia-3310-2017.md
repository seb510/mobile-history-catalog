In February 2017, HMD Global revived the Nokia 3310 name as part of its Nokia phone relaunch. The new button phone retained a recognizable silhouette while adding a colour screen, a camera, and a modern version of Snake. Its place in the timeline is a story of nostalgia turning an iconic design into a new product.

This entry covers the original 2G Dual SIM version. The separate Nokia 3310 3G and 4G models have different specifications. HMD announced an average global retail price of €49, not a US-dollar price. System software occupies part of the 16 MB internal storage.
