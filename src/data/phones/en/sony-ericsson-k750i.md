The 2005 K750i became a central camera phone in Sony Ericsson's range. Sony's report for the period highlighted its 2-megapixel camera alongside the music-focused W800. The pair shows how the brand gave related mobile capabilities different purposes.

- **Open the cover, take a picture.** Autofocus, a dedicated shutter key and an active lens cover made the camera quick to reach: opening the cover launched shooting. Held horizontally, the side controls sat on top, echoing a compact camera.
- **Know the generation.** Sony Ericsson's comparison with the later K800 shows the differences: LED illumination rather than xenon flash, Memory Stick Duo rather than smaller M2 cards, and GSM rather than the K800's added UMTS.

A music player and ordinary communication features complemented the camera. The K750i's place in the timeline is about making photography an accessible everyday tool, rather than simply an addition to calls. Comparing it with the W800i reveals a change of emphasis without treating the two phones as identical.
