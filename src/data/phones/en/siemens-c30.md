The Siemens C30 belongs to the company’s early generation of GSM candybars. A Siemens manual published in November 2000 describes a 110 g handset measuring 124 × 44 × 24 mm, operating on GSM 900/1800 networks.

Its distinctive detail is EasyClick: the front section, keypad and battery cover could be replaced to change the phone’s appearance without changing its electronics. The manual describes the C30’s basic phone functions—calls, SMS and a phone book—alongside simple games and a stopwatch.

For a comparison of Siemens designs, continue to the ME45 from the following year: the two show the difference between the C30’s straightforward candybar and the ME45’s protected body.
