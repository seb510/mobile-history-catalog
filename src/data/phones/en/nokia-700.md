At its 2011 launch, Nokia called the 700 the world’s most compact smartphone in its class. The 9.7 mm body paired a 3.2-inch AMOLED ClearBlack display with Symbian Belle and NFC. A 1 GHz processor powered the phone, and storage could be expanded with microSD.

The 5-megapixel camera with LED flash recorded HD video. The feature set fit into a 96 g handset, reflecting Nokia’s effort at the time to make a touchscreen smartphone compact and comfortable to hold in one hand.
