The 2003 T610 combined a colour display, built-in camera and aluminium body details. Ericsson's design history connects it with the Scandinavian tradition of simple forms and credits designer Erik Ahlgren. Its features became part of a coherent everyday object.

- **From photograph to message.** The camera and MMS created a short path from taking a picture to sharing it. Small photographs did not replace a separate camera, but making them no longer required an external camera accessory.
- **A personal organiser.** Bluetooth and infrared exchanged contacts, ringtones and calendar events. Documentation also described computer synchronisation, expanding the phone's role beyond calls.
