The Nokia 6280 brought a new mid-2000s habit into a familiar keypad phone: looking at the person on a video call. A VGA camera sits above the screen, a 2-megapixel camera faces backwards, and sliding the upper body reveals the number keys.

- A QVGA screen makes room for photos, video and Series 40 menus.
- miniSD carries music and pictures beyond the small internal storage.
- Bluetooth, infrared and Pop-Port recall an era of several competing ways to share a file.

Announced in June 2005, it was scheduled by Nokia for fourth-quarter shipments. Alongside Nseries smartphones, it offers another route into 3G: two cameras and a sliding body with a familiar feature-phone platform.
