The Nokia 8810 paired a chrome finish with a mechanical cover that slid down to reveal the keypad. Its reflective body and compact silhouette made it feel like a personal accessory as much as a communication device.

It was Nokia’s first premium phone and its first model with an internal antenna. The design removed the external stub from the phone’s outline, though the metal casing made reception more challenging. Nokia began deliveries in August 1998.
