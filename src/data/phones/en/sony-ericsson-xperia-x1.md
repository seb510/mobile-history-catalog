The Xperia X1 was Sony Ericsson’s attempt to pair a touchscreen with a phone built for messaging. Its side slider revealed a full QWERTY keyboard, and the mechanism followed an arc that tilted the screen slightly as it opened.

The wide 800 × 480 display and Windows Mobile 6.1 Professional gave it a different rhythm from the touchscreen candybars of the period. Supported bands varied by region, so this catalogue entry does not present one universal network configuration.
