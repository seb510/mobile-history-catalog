The 2019 Galaxy Fold was Samsung's first foldable smartphone. It attempted to change the geometry of a smartphone rather than simply enlarge its screen.

- **Two surfaces for different tasks.** The outer 4.6-inch display handled brief actions while closed. Unfolding the 7.3-inch inner screen offered more room for reading, video and multiple applications.
- **A revised construction.** Before sales began, Samsung extended the display's protective layer beyond the bezel, added protection around the hinge and reduced the gap between hinge and body. In July it prepared for a September launch after further testing. The first Fold's history includes these revisions as well as its presentation.

The technical portrait covers the LTE model with a typical 4,380 mAh battery; the separate 5G version had a different capacity. [Compare it with Fold3](/en/compare/?a=samsung-galaxy-fold&b=samsung-galaxy-z-fold3) to follow the development of Samsung's book-style foldable.
