The SL55 challenged Siemens' practical image with a compact sliding body, metallic-looking trim and a deep Ruby colour. It felt as much like a fashion accessory as a working phone.

- **The sliding gesture.** Its small body concealed the numeric keypad beneath the display. The mechanism gave this Siemens a character distinct from the brand's conventional candybars.
- **Colour, not material.** Ruby and Titan were colour names. Titan does not establish that the phone had a titanium body.

Despite limited memory and a small battery, the SL55 is remembered for its design. Siemens offered relatively few sliders, and this model expressed a more style-focused side of the brand.

Cable connectivity required a separate DCA-500 or DCA-510 accessory. Photography used an external QuickPic IQP-510 module rather than a built-in camera.
