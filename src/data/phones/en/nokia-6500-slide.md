The 6500 slide was Nokia’s metal-bodied slider: a restrained design paired with a 3.2 MP autofocus camera with Carl Zeiss optics. TV-out could send images to a compatible display.

Announced in May 2007, it is distinct from the Nokia 6500 classic, with a different mechanism, camera and multimedia focus.
