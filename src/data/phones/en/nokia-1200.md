Nokia designed the 1200 for households that might share a single handset. It offered five separate phonebooks and a way to set a call duration limit. A key turned on the flashlight, and the seamless keypad helped keep dust out.

Nokia announced it in 2007 at an estimated €35 before taxes and subsidies. Nokia Conversations later put sales at approximately 150 million units.
