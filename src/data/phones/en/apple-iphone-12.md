The iPhone 12 brought 5G across an entire iPhone generation. Its flat edges restored a distinctive silhouette, while MagSafe introduced a magnetic system for charging and accessories.

- **A generation-wide network change.** Support for 5G arrived throughout the range. This was an important step in wider adoption, rather than the first 5G smartphone on the market.
- **An attachment becomes an interaction.** MagSafe made magnetic alignment part of the charging and accessory experience, alongside the return to flat-sided industrial design.

In the archive, the iPhone 12 represents a later stage of smartphone development: changes in connectivity, form and accessories after touchscreens had become established.
