Mate XT 2 changes the original Mate XT’s gesture: the outer sections fold inward to protect the large flexible display. A separate cover screen supports closed-phone use.

Chinese sales began on 12 September 2026. Specifications describe the base 16/256 GB version with the standard 1–90 Hz inner display. The Privacy Display version has different specifications, including 1–120 Hz, and is not mixed into this portrait. Battery capacity is the typical 5600 mAh.
