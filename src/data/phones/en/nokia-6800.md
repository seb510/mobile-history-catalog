Closed, the Nokia 6800 resembles a conventional phone. Unfolding its front reveals two QWERTY halves around the display. The interface rotates 90 degrees and the device is held horizontally with both hands. Its mechanism changes not just the outline but the act of typing.

Announced in late 2002, it appears under 2003 in this catalog for its market release. Longer messages, notes and email formed its purpose. Java applications, PC Suite synchronisation and infrared complemented the physical keyboard.

These specifications describe the European NHL-6 with GSM 900/1800. The American announcement describes a different 850/1900 radio version; the bands are kept separate. In the photograph the 6800 is below the similar 6810, which is not another variant of this record.
