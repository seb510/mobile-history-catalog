The Microsoft Lumia 950 shipped with Windows 10 Mobile. A Display Dock let compatible apps run on a monitor with a keyboard and mouse while the phone remained available for calls and messages.

Its hardware combined USB-C, a removable battery and a PureView camera. In the archive, it represents the final generation of mobile Windows and its effort to bring phone and PC workflows closer together.
