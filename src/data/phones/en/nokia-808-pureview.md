The Nokia 808 PureView arrived in 2012 with a 41-megapixel sensor, Carl Zeiss optics and a xenon flash. In the default 5 MP mode, the phone combined information from multiple sensor pixels. Full-resolution images reached 38 MP at 4:3 or 34 MP at 16:9.

A physical shutter button and a prominent camera housing defined how the phone looked and handled. It ran Nokia Belle Feature Pack 1 and was Nokia’s final Symbian device, according to the company’s annual report.
