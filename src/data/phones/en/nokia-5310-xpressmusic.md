The Nokia 5310 XpressMusic put music controls on the body: dedicated keys, an audio chip and a 3.5 mm jack complemented its slim candybar shape. Unlike the 5300 slider, the music experience did not change the phone’s form.

Colour accents marked it as part of the XpressMusic range, while a microSD card held a personal music collection. This is the 2007 model, not the later revivals with the same name.
