On 17 March 2004 at CeBIT, Nokia introduced the 7610 as its first megapixel camera phone. Its asymmetric two-tone body emphasised creativity and personal style, while the familiar Series 60 platform ran inside.

- **A picture's next destination.** The camera captured up to 1152 × 864 pixels. Nokia described printing through compatible Bluetooth printers, transferring to photo kiosks and uploading to an online album through Kodak Pictures. Availability depended on markets and partners.
- **Creating a personal record.** Movie Director added music, text and effects to clips, while Lifeblog organised photographs, videos and messages as a digital diary. The phone became a tool for making and arranging personal media as well as capturing it.

Nokia planned a second-quarter 2004 release at approximately €500. That announcement estimate is not a universal dollar price. Compared with the Siemens S65, it represents the same move towards megapixel photography with a different emphasis: smartphone applications and expressive design.
