Under open sky, an arrow on the screen guided the user as they turned to keep a satellite in view. The phone sent their location, battery level and answers to a short questionnaire. A relay specialist could pass the details to emergency services that could only take voice calls. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111850).

Beyond cellular and Wi-Fi coverage, an iPhone 14 owner could answer a few questions and point the phone at a satellite to send rescuers their location and a short message. The service launched in the US and Canada in November.

[Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/11/emergency-sos-via-satellite-available-today-on-iphone-14-lineup/).
