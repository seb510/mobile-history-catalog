The P910i adds another gesture to stylus input: unfold the flip and type on a miniature QWERTY keyboard. Its outer face remains a numeric keypad.

64 MB user storage and Memory Stick PRO Duo support its work-oriented role. The manufacturer’s guide specifies 155 g including battery and flip.
