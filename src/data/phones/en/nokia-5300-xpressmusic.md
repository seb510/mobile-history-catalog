The Nokia 5300 XpressMusic paired a slider with music controls on the side. The sliding keypad served calls and text entry, while the music keys remained accessible without opening the body.

Announced in September 2006, the 5300 should not be confused with the Nokia 5200. Their silhouettes were similar, but the 5300 had its own trim and belonged to the XpressMusic line.
