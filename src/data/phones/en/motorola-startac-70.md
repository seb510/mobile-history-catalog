The StarTAC 70 extended Motorola’s idea of a compact phone with a folding earpiece, while offering a more affordable model in the range. The UK MR501 variant used GSM 1800; versions for other bands also existed, so network support depended on the specific variant.

The lower-tier model omitted the vibration alert and graphical display of the StarTAC 85. Its compact body and folding design still made carrying a phone in a pocket feel unusual for the time.
