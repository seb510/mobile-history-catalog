The P smart+ was the European name for a phone sold in Asia and the Middle East as the Huawei
Nova 3i: the same body and the same specifications under different names for different markets.
It launched in Asia in July 2018 and was announced in Europe on August 2, 2018 as part of the
refreshed P smart line.

Its main feature was dual cameras on both sides: 16 + 2MP on the back with a depth sensor for
portrait mode, and 24 + 2MP on the front for the same kind of portrait shots from a selfie
camera. At launch, that was a notable set of capabilities for a mid-range phone.

European sales opened at roughly €299. No separate USD price was officially announced, since the
launch price was only given in euros for the European market.
