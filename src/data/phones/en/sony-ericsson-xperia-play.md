Closed, the Xperia PLAY worked as a touchscreen smartphone. Sliding the display sideways uncovered a directional pad, four PlayStation symbol buttons and two circular touch pads. Players could feel the directions under their thumb and keep their fingers clear of the picture.

Support for the gamepad depended on the game. That distinction gives the PLAY its place in Xperia history: Sony Ericsson put physical controls in an Android handset and gave developers a chance to design for them.
