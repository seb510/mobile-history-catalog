The W350i’s screen stayed in place. Only the thin panel covering the number keys flipped down. Playback controls on its outer face let you pause music or skip a song while the keypad stayed covered.

To write a message, you lowered the panel and typed on the number keys. A 512 MB M2 card came in the box, and Media Manager could transfer songs from a computer over USB.
