The original Pixel introduced Google's smartphone line under a new name. Its defining idea was a close connection between the device and Google's services, with Google Assistant built in from the start.

Source: [Google — Pixel](https://blog.google/intl/en-mena/product-updates/explore-get-answers/2016-google-assistant-en/).
