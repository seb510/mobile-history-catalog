The 5130 XpressMusic is a budget entry in Nokia's music sub-brand: dedicated player keys (play/pause, track skip) sit below the screen, and two built-in loudspeakers let you listen without headphones. It's the same idea as the XpressMusic flagships, just in a plain candybar body with no slider or swivel mechanism.

The rest of the hardware is typical of a 2009 S40 phone: a 2-inch QVGA display, a 2-megapixel camera with no flash, 30 MB of built-in storage with a bundled 1 GB microSD card, a 3.5mm audio jack and microUSB. The removable BL-5C battery is rated at 1020 mAh.

The maximum supported microSD capacity and the exact Bluetooth profile set reflect the sources available at the time of writing; any other official limits Nokia may have published are not confirmed here.
