The K700i brought photography, video clips, MP3 playback and FM radio together in a compact keypad phone. Compared with the T610, the emphasis shifted from simply having a camera to a broader set of image and audio features.

Its camera captured VGA images at 640 × 480 and had an illumination light. Extended mode produced 1280 × 960 images, but the manufacturer explained that this was an enlarged frame. A larger software output does not mean a megapixel sensor. The illumination light should not be confused with the xenon flash found on later models.

The media player handled music and video, the radio stored favourite stations, and Bluetooth and infrared transferred files between devices. In the timeline, the model represents the convergence of phone, player and pocket camera.
