The first Galaxy Note was announced at IFA on 1 September 2011. Its 5.3-inch screen and S Pen offered more room for reading, annotation and drawing while retaining mobile communication. It became an important milestone for large smartphones known as phablets.

- **Handwriting as interaction.** The stylus was part of how the phone worked, rather than simply an accessory for a large screen. The first S Pen recognised 256 pressure levels.
- **A shortcut to a note.** Double-tapping while holding the pen button opened S Memo, avoiding a trip through the application menu.

The Note matters for combining a large working surface with handwriting. Features from later Notes should not all be attributed to the original: the series developed gradually.
