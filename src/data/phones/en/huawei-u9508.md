Honor 2 is the market name for the model with the factory designation Huawei U9508 (also known as Honor Quad). It isn't the Ascend D1 Quad — that phone carries the U9500/U9510 designation and is a separate device from the same 2012 wave. Honor 2 was one of the first quad-core Android smartphones under Honor, the sub-brand Huawei had just split off from its main Ascend line.

At its core sits Huawei's own quad-core K3V2 chipset (HiSilicon Hi3620, Cortex-A9 at 1.4 GHz) paired with 2 GB of RAM — an unusually generous amount for its class at the time. The 4.5-inch IPS display runs at 720 × 1280, and the main camera shoots 8 MP stills with 1080p video. 8 GB of built-in storage can be extended with a microSD card up to 32 GB. The 2230 mAh battery is removable.

The phone launched in China in the fourth quarter of 2012 running Android 4.0 (Ice Cream Sandwich); it has no NFC. No freely licensed photo of this model was found on Wikimedia Commons or in the Mobile Phone Museum collection, so this entry is currently without an image.
