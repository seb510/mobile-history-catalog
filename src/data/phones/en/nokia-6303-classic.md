The Nokia 6303 classic carried forward the design of the [Nokia 6300](/en/phones/nokia-6300/): a slim body with stainless-steel details, a number pad below the screen, and a central navigation key. The 2009 model rounded off the corners and grew the display from 2 to 2.2 inches.

Its 3.2 MP camera gained autofocus and a dual LED flash. You could load a microSD card with MP3s and plug ordinary wired headphones into the 3.5 mm jack for music and FM radio. The [Nokia 6700 classic](/en/phones/nokia-6700-classic/) from the same lineup offered 3G, while the 6303 went online over EDGE.
