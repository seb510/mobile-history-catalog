The 2008 E71 centres on an always-accessible QWERTY keyboard and a landscape display. Its 10 mm body keeps the full alphabet under the fingers without opening or rotating a mechanism — a different approach from the Siemens SK65's concealed keyboard.

- **Shortcuts to work.** Dedicated keys open email, calendar and contacts. Two customisable home screens switch between work and personal tasks.
- **Connected beyond the desk.** Nokia listed Mail for Exchange, POP/IMAP, Wi-Fi and A-GPS. Network-service availability depended on carrier and configuration.

A 3.2 MP camera, music player and radio complemented its business role. The E71 shows how a physical keyboard could define a slim smartphone alongside multimedia features.
