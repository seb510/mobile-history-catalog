The Xelibri 2 was conceived not as a miniature business tool but as a conspicuous accessory. Its rounded, aerodynamic lines and unusual keypad set it apart from the familiar rectangular candybar.

An integrated clip let the phone attach to a collar, cuff or bag; a separate fitting allowed it to hang from a lanyard. Siemens called it “The Alien Beauty,” emphasizing style and the way it was worn. Its listed weight was 73 g.
