The PEBL U6 was shaped around the idea of a smooth pebble: a rounded body, soft-touch finish and a narrow outer display window. Beside the sharp lines of the RAZR, it offered a different way to make a clamshell recognisable.

- **One-handed opening.** The hinge mechanism allowed the phone to open with one hand. The feel of opening it was part of its identity, alongside the rounded silhouette.
- **Small-scale multimedia.** Motorola's developer sheet listed a VGA camera, video recording, Bluetooth and mini-USB. Around 5 MB of user memory suited short clips, ringtones and messages rather than a large media archive.

In 2006, Motorola expanded the idea with PEBL Colors and brighter finishes. That later update should not be confused with the original U6's 2005 debut.
