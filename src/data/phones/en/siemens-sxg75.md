SXG75 marked a final technology milestone for Siemens Mobile in 2005: an in-house UMTS phone with GPS, a QVGA display, two cameras for photographs and video calls, and RS-MMC expansion. It suggested a future built around mobile internet, navigation, and multimedia.

It is often remembered as the “last Siemens.” More precisely, it was the company’s final in-house UMTS handset: the planned SG75 was cancelled, while the next chapter belonged to BenQ-Siemens. This distinction avoids calling it the last phone ever to bear the Siemens name.
