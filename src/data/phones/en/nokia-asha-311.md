The Asha 311 showed how Nokia adapted Series 40 to affordable touchscreen phones in 2012. Its three-inch capacitive display was designed for finger input. Asha Touch gestures let users move between menus, shortcuts and apps.

Wi-Fi and 3G provided internet access, and Nokia Browser compressed pages to save mobile data. A 3.2-megapixel camera and microSD support covered everyday photography and music.
