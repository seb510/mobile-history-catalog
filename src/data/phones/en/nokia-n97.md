Closed, the Nokia N97 was a touchscreen smartphone with a 3.5-inch display. Slide the top half sideways and the hinge lifted the screen at an angle, exposing the QWERTY keyboard. On a desk it resembled a tiny laptop, and the navigation pad to the left of the keyboard let you move through menus without touching the screen.

In 2009, Nokia put widgets on the home screen. Weather, calendar entries and shortcuts could sit one tap away from standby. The 32 GB of built-in storage made room for a large music library, and a microSD card could hold more photos and video.
