The Xelibri 8 closed the brand’s second collection as a pendant phone. It was designed to be worn on the chain supplied with the device; the handset itself weighed 67 g.

There was more beneath the accessory-like exterior: the phone supported voice control and included a VHF radio for music. The Xelibri 8 came in Diamond pink and Silver glam, blending communication with a piece of jewellery.
