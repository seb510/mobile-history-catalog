The N90 changes shape with the task: a conventional clamshell for calls, a rotating camera barrel for photographs and a twisted main display for video. Opening and turning the hardware becomes part of its interface.

Announced on 27 April 2005, it pairs a 2MP Carl Zeiss autofocus camera with a 352 × 416 main screen and a 128 × 128 cover display. Its 31 MB of internal memory and bundled 64 MB RS-MMC are listed separately. Its place in this archive comes from that changing grip and silhouette.
