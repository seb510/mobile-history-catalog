razr ultra brings the compact flip gesture into the touchscreen era. Its four-inch cover screen runs apps while closed; a seven-inch display opens inside.

Orient Blue Alcantara and Cocoa wood veneer continue Motorola’s interest in tactile materials. US sales began on 21 May 2026; other markets call it razr 70 ultra. It is distinct from razr ultra 2025 and the standard razr 2026.

Sources: [Motorola — razr family 2026 announcement](https://motorolanews.com/power-style-and-flexibility-motorola-introduces-three-new-flip-devices-to-the-razr-family/); [Motorola — razr ultra 2026](https://www.motorola.com/us/en/p/phones/razr/razr-ultra-2026/pmipmjk44m3).

Memory configurations vary by market: the official page describes 256/512 GB storage and 12/16 GB physical RAM without establishing one universal pairing here. RAM Boost uses storage and is not added to physical RAM. Charging figures are maximum supported rates with compatible equipment.
