The Galaxy Z Fold3 5G showed how Samsung was trying to make its book-style foldable more practical for everyday use. In 2021 it added IPX8 water resistance, support for dedicated S Pens and an under-display camera on the inner screen. These changes were about more than specification numbers: they addressed durability, handwriting and using the large display without a permanent camera cutout.

The 7.6-inch inner display could run several apps at once. Multi-Active Window and saved app pairs could, for example, put messaging alongside a calendar; Flex mode used the partly folded body as a distinct working position. These scenarios depended on app compatibility.

S Pen support did not mean a stylus was included. Sold separately, the pen worked only on the inner display; compatible options were the S Pen Fold Edition and S Pen Pro. A regular stylus from another Galaxy Note should not be assumed to work. Likewise, IPX8 describes water resistance, not verified dust protection.

The Fold3 matters as an attempt to combine a smartphone with a workspace for notes and multiple windows. It makes an interesting comparison with the Mate XT: both expand the screen by unfolding the body, but use different numbers of folds and support different scenarios.
