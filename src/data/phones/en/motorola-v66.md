The V66 shows Motorola's clamshell before the RAZR era: a short rounded body, monochrome display and keypad hidden beneath the lid. Its story centres on calls, messages and organisation rather than photography.

- **Small-screen tools.** The manual described voice tags, Voice Notes, a calendar and iTAP predictive input. The Smart button changed function with context, such as confirming a menu choice. Adjustable text-line counts balanced information density with readability.
- **Radio through an accessory.** The radio menu appeared only with the optional FM Stereo Radio Headset. A menu label did not mean a built-in FM receiver; accessory-dependent features shaped the experience.
