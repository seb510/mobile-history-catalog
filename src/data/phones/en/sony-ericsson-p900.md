With the keypad closed, you could dial the P900 one-handed. Flipping it down exposed the lower part of the touchscreen. That was the moment to take out the stylus, tap a date in the calendar and enter an appointment.

The P900 illustrates how different Symbian smartphones could feel. UIQ accommodated precise stylus input, while a side-mounted Jog Dial let you scroll through lists with your thumb.

According to Sony Ericsson's own technical data, the battery delivered up to 13 hours of talk time and up to 400 hours on standby. The phone had only a basic VGA camera with no flash or zoom — adequate in 2003 for simple photos and MMS. A Memory Stick Duo slot (a 32 MB card was included) extended the 16 MB of built-in user storage.
