The 6230i kept the understated shape of the Nokia 6230 while updating its everyday focal points: a 208 × 208-pixel square screen and a 1.3-megapixel camera. It remained a small keypad phone that could play music and exchange files over Bluetooth, infrared or a cable.

Its character comes from bringing work and entertainment together without moving to a smartphone:

- Removable MMC cards carried MP3, M4A and AAC tracks.
- FM radio complemented a personal music collection.
- The replaceable BL-5C battery stayed accessible beneath the back cover.

Nokia’s 2005 report identifies the 6230i as its highest revenue-generating phone that year. That documents commercial importance; it is not a disclosed unit-sales figure.
