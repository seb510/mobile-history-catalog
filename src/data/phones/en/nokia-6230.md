Nokia announced the 6230 on 28 October 2003, with shipments planned for the first quarter of 2004. The catalogue therefore uses 2004. It addressed mobile professionals seeking a compact phone for both work and personal use.

- **Multimedia in a familiar body.** A numeric keypad concealed a VGA camera with video recording, an MP3 player, removable MMC storage and Bluetooth. Users could carry music and photographs and use wireless accessories without a large smartphone body.
- **EDGE, not 3G.** EDGE improved data transfer on compatible networks. Frequency variants depended on the market; they should not be combined into a universal specification.

The 6230's value lay in combining capabilities, rather than establishing a first for each: MMC support does not make it the first phone with a memory card. Alongside the Siemens S65 from the same year, it shows a different balance of business features, compactness and multimedia; the S65 placed more emphasis on its megapixel camera.
