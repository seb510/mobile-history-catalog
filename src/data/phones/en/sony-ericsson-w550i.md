W550i made a music phone something to turn in your hands. Its Walkman front swivels relative to the keypad, while replaceable Style-Up covers change its appearance. Sony Ericsson introduced W550 in August 2005 as a combination of music, photography and games.

A dedicated key opens the player, and play/pause controls tracks without navigating menus. Disc2Phone transferred music from a computer into 256 MB of built-in memory: a small personal library, with song capacity dependent on format and bitrate.

Gaming had its own physical arrangement: a landscape display with buttons on either side for two-handed control, plus portrait and L-shaped options. Beside Nokia 5700, it shows a different approach to musical mechanics: a rotating front instead of a twisting lower block.
