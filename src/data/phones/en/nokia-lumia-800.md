The Lumia 800 brought the Nokia N9’s recognisable silhouette to a new line. A coloured polycarbonate body and curved glass frame an AMOLED screen where large tiles replace the familiar icon grid.

It shipped with Windows Phone 7.5 Mango. People Hub grouped contacts and conversations, and Nokia added turn-by-turn navigation through Nokia Drive.
