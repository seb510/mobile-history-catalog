The KG800 Chocolate is built around waking up: its smooth front comes alive with red touch-sensitive controls. It is not a touchscreen smartphone. A conventional number keypad slides out for calls and messages. Gloss, light and movement define this international Black Label model.

- The MP3 player uses 128 MB of internal storage.
- A 1.3-megapixel camera and Bluetooth complete the package.
- The manual explains touch-key locking and recommends using clean, dry fingertips.

LG reported one million Chocolate sales within eight weeks of the international launch. This entry is specifically the 2006 GSM KG800. The American CDMA VX8500 has a different body and its own archive page.
