A 1.1-inch cover screen showed the time and doubled as a viewfinder for selfies with the rear camera. With the phone half open, the camera app placed the viewfinder on the top half and kept the shutter button within reach below.

The Galaxy Z Flip incorporated ultra-thin glass beneath the flexible display surface. Its hinge held the screen partly open, so you could set the phone on a table and frame a hands-free shot.
