The 8800 builds its character from material and movement: cool steel, smooth surfaces and a short slide before dialling. Announced on 7 April 2005, it used a spring mechanism and ball bearings to uncover the keypad and the concealed camera.

Ryuichi Sakamoto composed its ringtones and alerts. This entry covers the original 8800; later Sirocco and Arte specifications belong to different models. It earns its place here through the feel of opening and holding it.
