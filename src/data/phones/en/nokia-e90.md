Closed, the **Nokia E90 Communicator** looked like a candybar phone, with a colour screen, a navigation key and a numeric keypad. The case opened sideways like a book to reveal an 800 × 352 display and a QWERTY keyboard you could type on with both hands.

Earlier Communicators used Series 80 for the inner interface. Nokia moved the E90 to **S60 on both screens**. Open the case while using an app on the outer display and it would continue on the inner screen. You could check an email on the move, then open up the keyboard to write a reply.

Quickoffice could open Word documents, Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint presentations. Email and web browsing worked over Wi‑Fi or 3G with HSDPA, and built-in GPS worked with maps. The two displays and hinged case made for a substantial handset: it weighed 210 g and measured 20 mm thick when closed.
