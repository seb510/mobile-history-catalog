Closed, the E90 has a numeric keypad; opened, it offers a wide display and two-handed QWERTY keyboard. The change of posture joins telephone and work tool.

The 2007 Communicator moved from Series 80 to S60, adding Wi-Fi, HSDPA and GPS. Its listed 128 MB is user storage, not RAM.
