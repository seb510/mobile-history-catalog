The M65 brought Siemens' 2004 multimedia features into a body intended for more active use. Its metal frame and protective elements were part of the construction, not just styling around the menu. The manual gives assembly of the body and installation of the frame their own steps, making correct assembly relevant to everyday use.

The phone still had a color display, camera and familiar series features such as messaging, an organizer and downloadable applications. Its purpose was not simply to be a “hard-to-break phone for calls”: it offered familiar multimedia scenarios in a distinctly different enclosure.

Protective styling does not mean unlimited resistance to water, impact or dust. The frame and protective elements complemented the usual care needed with electronic devices. As with other camera phones of the period, owners still had to look after the body, connectors and covers.

Compared with the ME45, the M65 shows how this branch of Siemens developed: a practical enclosure gained color imagery, photography and new ways to exchange content. Unlike the business-focused S65, the M65 put more emphasis on its body and on use away from the office.
