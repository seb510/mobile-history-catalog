The K810i continued the K800i’s camera-first idea with a different tactile identity: individual round keys, a joystick and a slimmer lens cover. From the front it is a recognisable phone; a landscape grip and a physical shutter button turn the back into a small camera.

- Its 3.2-megapixel camera combines autofocus with a xenon flash.
- Photo fix adjusts brightness and contrast after a picture is taken.
- Illuminated key markings provide camera shortcuts, while M2 cards add photo storage.

Introduced in 2007, it developed the established Cyber-shot family. Compare it with the same-year W910i to see one manufacturer give photography and music different bodies and controls.
