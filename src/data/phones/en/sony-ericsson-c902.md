The C902 kept its keypad exposed. Pulling the top of the handset revealed the lens and launched the camera. Held sideways, the phone put a dedicated shutter button under the index finger like a compact camera.

A silver C902 appeared in Quantum of Solace, where Bond uses it to photograph people at the opera. A tie-in edition included a spy game and 007 material on the memory card.

Opening the lens cover lit up eight blue touch controls around the display. These let the photographer change focus mode, set the timer or switch from stills to video. The physical shutter button remained under the index finger to take the shot.
