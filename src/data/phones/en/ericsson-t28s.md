The Ericsson T28s packed a GSM phone into a body just 15 mm thick and weighing 83 g. Its keypad cover opened at the press of a side button, making the gesture part of the phone’s character rather than merely a way to protect the keys.

The phone supported voice control and stored up to 99 contacts in its own memory, separate from entries on the SIM card. Ericsson announced the T28 early in 1999; sales began later that year.
