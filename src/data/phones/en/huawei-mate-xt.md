The Mate XT Ultimate Design develops the foldable smartphone through three screen sections and two hinges. Fully unfolded, its display measures 10.2 inches. The catalogue's 2024 date refers to the original Chinese model; global Huawei materials help explain the construction, but regional versions should not be mixed without checking.

- **More than open or closed.** Unlike a single-fold book-style phone, intermediate unfolding provides another working surface. Reading, documents and multiple windows can use different screen sizes rather than one fixed expanded format.
- **Versatility with complexity.** The thin unfolded dimensions do not describe the folded phone's thickness, and the large diagonal does not mean it is always used as a tablet. These distinctions matter more than comparing one diagonal with an ordinary smartphone.

The Mate XT expands the form factor into several screen-size configurations. The commercial tri-fold priority claim belongs to Huawei; it does not mean the construction has become a mass-market standard.
