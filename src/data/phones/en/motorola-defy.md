The original 2010 Defy paired Android with a body intended for tougher everyday conditions. Motorola's December Singapore announcement emphasised resistance to water, dust and scratches while retaining a conventional 3.7-inch touchscreen form.

- **Practical during calls.** Corning Gorilla Glass protected the display surface, while CrystalTalk PLUS used two microphones to address background noise. Practicality extended beyond the body to calls in noisy surroundings, the web, photographs and messages.
- **Protection has limits.** Resistance did not imply surviving every fall or arbitrary immersion. Manufacturer materials described everyday splashes, spilled drinks and sand, rather than an industrial tool suited to any conditions.

This page concerns the 2010 Defy. Later devices named motorola defy have different hardware and protection conditions. Alongside the Siemens M65, it continues the idea of a practical phone into the Android, application and touchscreen era.
