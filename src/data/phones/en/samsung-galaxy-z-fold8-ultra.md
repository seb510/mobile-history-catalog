Fold8 Ultra continues the book-style body: a narrow phone opens into an eight-inch workspace. The hinge becomes its defining physical gesture, while the camera brings the foldable closer to the Ultra family.

Sales began on 7 August 2026. The table describes the base 12/256 GB configuration and main display; the cover screen is 6.5 inches at 1080 × 2520. Battery capacity is typical. This is Fold8 Ultra, not the standard Fold8.

Sources: [Samsung — Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra announcement](https://www.samsungmobilepress.com/articles/galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-fold8-flip8-perfected-every-way-living); [Samsung — availability from 7 August 2026](https://samsungmobilepress.com/articles/galaxy-z-fold8-ultra-flip8-watch-ultra2-watch9-official-launch).

Table dimensions describe the folded phone; unfolded dimensions are 158.4 × 143.2 × 4.1 mm. The operating system is shown at launch.
