Announced on 25 February 2013, the Lumia 520 was then Nokia's most affordable Windows Phone 8. The €139 estimate excluded taxes and subsidies, with initial shipments planned for March in Hong Kong and Vietnam. It was not a universal dollar price.

- **Familiar Lumia features for less.** A sensitive 4-inch display worked with gloves, interchangeable coloured covers changed its appearance, and Nokia Music and HERE complemented the system. Service availability depended on country and terms.
- **Room for personal files.** 512 MB RAM, 8 GB storage and microSD support up to 64 GB prioritised an affordable smartphone over flagship performance. The autofocus camera recorded 720p video; photography, music and navigation formed part of the promise.

Launch software must be distinguished from later updates. The original announcement listed Bluetooth 3.0; later software capabilities should not be assigned to the first handsets. The 520 sought to broaden Windows Phone's audience through price and services without requiring an unsupported sales superlative.
