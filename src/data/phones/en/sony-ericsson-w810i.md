The W810i continued the W800i formula: a button-operated phone, music player and camera in one body. Connectivity changes included quad-band GSM and EDGE. EDGE improved data transfer on compatible networks; it did not make this a 3G phone.

- **A removable library.** Music used Memory Stick PRO Duo. Documentation specified a 512 MB card — PRO Duo, not the smaller M2. Removable storage took a collection beyond the limited internal memory.
- **Music without abandoning photography.** A 2 MP camera offered autofocus and illumination. The model refined a specialised series, adding connectivity while retaining photography alongside its familiar music features.
