The C72 continued Siemens' budget C-series candybar line: built on the C65's body, it added a built-in VGA camera at a price point where cameras had only recently stopped being an afterthought or an optional extra. There's no video recording — stills only.

A CSTN 128 × 128 display shows 65,000 colors, and Java MIDP 2.0 powers downloadable apps and games. There's no Bluetooth — connecting to a computer means the infrared port or a USB cable. The phone was announced in August 2005.
