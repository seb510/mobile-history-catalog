The W610i brought the recognisable Walkman language to a compact candybar: orange accents, a music player and a memory-card listening setup gave it a different mood from the camera-focused K550i.

It also had a 2 MP autofocus camera with an LED flash. This is a separate 2007 Walkman model, not a K550i with a different case colour.
