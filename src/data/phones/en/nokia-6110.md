Nokia announced the 6110 in December 1997 and began volume shipments in early 1998. The handset had a short antenna, a monochrome display, a calendar, a calculator and three games: Snake, Memory and Logic.

The 6110 was Nokia’s first phone with Snake. The built-in infrared port let two people play on separate handsets by pointing their ports at each other.
