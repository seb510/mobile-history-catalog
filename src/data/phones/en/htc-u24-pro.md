U24 pro represents HTC’s later upper range, combining curved glass with a bevelled aluminium frame. Practical details survive alongside the touchscreen: microSD expansion and a 3.5 mm headphone jack.

Its Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 is not a Snapdragon 8-class flagship chip. The model illustrates HTC’s changed positioning after Dream and its major flagship years. Specifications refer to the UK 12/256 GB version and launch operating system.
