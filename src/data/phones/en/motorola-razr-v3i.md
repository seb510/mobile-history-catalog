The RAZR V3i retained the V3's thin metal body while expanding its multimedia capabilities. It developed the familiar design rather than replacing its central idea.

- **More room for pictures and music.** The camera offered 1.23 MP and video recording. A microSD card allowed music and photographs to be stored on removable media.
- **Two music-player options.** Motorola described either iTunes or its own Digital Audio Music Player, depending on version and market. A flight mode allowed listening without cellular service; mini-USB and Bluetooth served accessories and data transfer.

The manufacturer's fact sheet planned availability for the fourth quarter of 2005; the catalogue currently retains 2006. Those dates should not be treated as equivalent: a manufacturer's plan alone does not establish actual availability in every country. [Compare it with the V3](/en/compare/?a=motorola-razr-v3&b=motorola-razr-v3i) to see how a familiar design gained expandable storage and a new camera.
