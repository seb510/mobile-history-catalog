With the N91 closed, the playback controls sat under your thumb. You could plug your own headphones into the 3.5 mm jack and skip tracks. To dial a number, you slid the music panel down to reveal the number pad.

The original model kept its music library on a miniature 4 GB hard drive. Nokia announced it alongside the N70 and N90 in 2005. Its catalogue year is 2006, when it went on sale. An 8 GB edition with twice the storage followed; neither version has a memory card slot.

The phone supported both GSM and third-generation UMTS networks, plus Wi-Fi — an unusual combination for 2006. The BL-5C battery is removable, and the 0.3 MP front camera was meant for video calls over 3G.
