The 7600 reshapes a familiar handset into an almost square object with keys around its display. Nokia introduced it at London’s 100% Design exhibition in September 2003.

WCDMA, imaging and music sat behind that unusual face. Its grip and button placement make early 3G feel distinctly physical.
