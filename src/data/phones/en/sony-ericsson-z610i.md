With the Z610i closed, the outline of its outer screen was almost invisible. A single-line 128 × 36 OLED sat beneath the translucent finish. When a call came in, the caller’s details appeared through the lid so you could decide whether to flip it open.

Inside were the colour screen, navigation pad and number keys. The camera lens remained on the outside, and pictures could be saved to a removable M2 card.
