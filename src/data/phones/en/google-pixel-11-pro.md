Pixel 11 Pro continues the recognisable horizontal camera composition. In the modern part of this archive, its character comes from the camera bar, image processing and magnetic Pixelsnap accessories.

This is the smaller Pro, not Pro XL: the base configuration has 256 GB storage and 12 GB RAM; 512 GB and 1 TB versions have 16 GB RAM. Battery capacity is typical; SIM specifications refer to the US. Seven years of updates are promised.
