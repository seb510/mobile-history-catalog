The T68i captures a transition between the colour phone and the camera phone. It had no built-in camera, but supported an external CommuniCam. Its April 2002 white paper explicitly described the MCA-20 and the ability to view, save and send photographs from the phone.

- **Colour and messages.** A 256-colour display supported MMS, themes and image sharing. Multimedia messages combined different kinds of content, but sending them depended on the carrier's service and correct settings.
- **A separate camera.** The camera was an accessory, not a standard module inside the handset. Its place outside the body is central to understanding this generation.

Bluetooth, infrared and contact/calendar synchronisation connected the T68i with other devices. Its significance here lies in combining colour, messaging and accessories. [Compare it with the T610](/en/compare/?a=sony-ericsson-t68i&b=sony-ericsson-t610) to see the next step: a camera inside the body.
