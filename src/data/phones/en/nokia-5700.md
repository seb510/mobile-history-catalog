Nokia announced the 5700 on 29 March 2007, targeting Q2 availability at about €350 before taxes and subsidies. It described four twist-selected modes: music player, camera, video call and smartphone. Rotating the hardware physically changed the task.

The phone also captures the era before streaming dominated mobile music: Nokia emphasised downloaded purchases from different stores and audio-format compatibility. Unlike a standalone MP3 player, it combined a music library with 3G and installable apps. Actual song capacity depended on file sizes and encoding.


5700 XpressMusic developed the Nokia 3250 concept: its lower module rotated to expose numeric or music controls and change the camera direction. The mechanism was part of the interface. A dedicated audio chip, stereo speakers and Bluetooth stereo supported its music focus.

Symbian enabled installable applications, while 3G supported mobile services and video calls. Standard 3.5 mm headphones connected through the supplied AD-44 adapter, rather than a built-in 3.5 mm socket.
