5700 XpressMusic developed the Nokia 3250 concept: its lower module rotated to expose numeric or music controls and change the camera direction. The mechanism was part of the interface. A dedicated audio chip, stereo speakers and Bluetooth stereo supported its music focus.

Symbian enabled installable applications, while 3G supported mobile services and video calls. Standard 3.5 mm headphones connected through the supplied AD-44 adapter, rather than a built-in 3.5 mm socket.
