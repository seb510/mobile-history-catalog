The iPhone 13 Pro brought Apple's adaptive ProMotion display from its tablets into the Pro iPhone
line: the screen adjusts its refresh rate from 10 to 120Hz depending on content, instead of the
fixed 60Hz of earlier models. It was the first iPhone with the technology.

The triple camera system gained a macro mode and a wider optical zoom range (3x telephoto, up to
6x combined with the ultra-wide lens), while a new Cinematic mode added automatic refocusing
during video recording. The A15 Bionic chip and a larger battery extended battery life over the
iPhone 12 Pro.

US sales opened at $999 for the 128GB tier; pre-orders began on September 17, with availability
starting September 24, 2021. Graphite, gold, silver and Sierra Blue were available at launch;
Alpine Green and green were added in spring 2022.
