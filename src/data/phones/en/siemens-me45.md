The ME45 combined Siemens' business-phone capabilities with a body designed for active use. The manufacturer positioned it alongside the S45, with GPRS, an organiser and data exchange over infrared. Protection from dust and splashes added practicality beyond the office.

In the catalog, the ME45 represents a period when a mobile phone was also becoming a work tool: packet data, WAP and synchronisation extended its calling and messaging functions.

Source: [Original Siemens S45 / ME45 brochure, 2001](https://manuals.plus/m/8a88f615163f2930749c085e2cc95692459ccb04afc7a3f5af11c544b0dc43f6).
The ME45 is listed with an 840 mAh Li-ion battery, 99 g weight and 109 × 46 × 21 mm dimensions; the neighbouring S45's figures are not substituted.
