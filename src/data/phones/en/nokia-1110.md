Nokia aimed the 1110 at people buying their first mobile phone. Demo mode walked through basic functions, and the inverted display showed light characters against a dark background. The handset focused on calls, texts and prepaid service. With operator support, Nokia Prepaid Tracker reported the remaining balance after a call or message.

Nokia Conversations later put sales at approximately 250 million units. Straightforward controls and everyday features helped make this basic keypad phone a mass-market device.
