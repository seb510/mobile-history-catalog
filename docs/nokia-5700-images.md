Author: William J Sisti. Photographs dated 30 June 2007, sourced from Wikimedia Commons:

- [Front](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nokia_5700_(666485189).jpg)
- [Music mode](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nokia_5700_front_music_(666493751).jpg)

All four local images in `public/images/phones/nokia-5700/` are distributed under [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). Original authorship remains William J Sisti. Music, camera and original-front photographs were converted to WebP without retouching. The main `front-cutout-v1.webp` is an AI-processed derivative by the Mobile History project, followed by WebP encoding. Its credit explicitly identifies AI processing in all three languages. The unprocessed front photograph remains in the gallery for comparison.

The generated cutout can reconstruct small details; it is an editorial adaptation, not documentary evidence of key legends, screen pixels or exact geometry. Other gallery frames are the historical photographs.

## Gallery revision

At the user’s request, the camera-mode frame was replaced in all three locales with [Nokia 5700 XpressMusic](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nokia_5700_XpressMusic.jpg), by 阿貴, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The remote original is used without editing. The local camera file remains an unused pilot asset. The main image is unchanged.

## Generation record

Mode: built-in image generation tool, image edit, transparent background. Input: original front photograph above. WebP encoding used Sharp, quality 86, without resizing.

Exact prompt (the removed neighbouring object was described as an iPod remote in the prompt; its identity is irrelevant to the retained handset):

> Edit target: the supplied real archival photograph by William J Sisti, showing a white/black Nokia 5700 XpressMusic on the right and a white iPod remote on the left. Task: precisely isolate ONLY the actual Nokia handset from that photo on a genuinely transparent background for an archival technology website. Remove the iPod remote, perforated metal desk, and all surrounding objects. This is a conservative background-removal edit, NOT a product redesign or invented studio recreation. Preserve the handset's exact existing front-facing perspective, outline, white/black/silver colours, proportions, button count, every key legend, Nokia wordmark, screen content and small wear marks. Preserve the original dim screen; do not invent a new wallpaper, brighten it into a different interface, replace text, reconstruct symbols, or cosmetically modernise any detail. Do not rotate, distort, slim, add accessories, add highlights, or change the physical construction. Keep the entire phone including all rounded edges and its bottom fully visible. Centre it on a portrait canvas, with the handset occupying about 88 percent of image height and enough transparent space on all sides. No text outside the handset, no labels, no gradients, no floor, no drop shadow, no frame. Clean accurate alpha edges; retain authentic photographic detail rather than reillustrating it.
