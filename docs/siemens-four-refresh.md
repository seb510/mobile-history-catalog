# Siemens A65 → CX65 → CX75 → SXG75

Refresh of four existing records, not new catalog models. Ukrainian, Russian and English content is now complete for each, including newly translated CX65 files. Reading routes and comparison links follow A65 → CX65 → CX75 → SXG75, with SXG75 looking back at CX75. The user's personal ownership history motivated the work but is not published as a biographical claim.

## Technical/editorial corrections

- **A65:** [user guide](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/152396/Siemens-A65.html), [manufacturer service features](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/2120476/Siemens-A65-Puma.html?page=9). Replaced the former 75 g with 85 g, which appears in manufacturer documentation. Dimensions conflict between the service sheet (100 × 44 × 19 mm) and other Siemens guides (110 × 47 × 23 mm), so the former unsourced 101 × 45 × 19 value was removed pending resolution. Capacity remains 700 mAh; conflicting chemistry/accessory nomenclature is not shown as a definitive battery model. Explicitly distinguishes no built-in camera from the optional QuickPic Camera IQP-500, with cable data via optional DCA accessories. Removed exact release-day, contact-limit and anecdotal camera-price claims that were not supported by the reviewed primary sources.
- **CX65:** [manufacturer user guide](https://altehandys.de/downloads/man-si-cx65.pdf) and [contemporary Siemens comparison table in the SL65 service document](https://firmware.center/firmware/Siemens/SL65%20%28Iris%29/Service%20Docs/SL65%20SVC%20Manual_V1.1.pdf). The user guide documents CLIPIt covers, VGA stills, video, optional external flash and digital-zoom limitations. Removed the unsupported “record-large display” superlative. Retained 108 × 46 × 18 mm: the service comparison gives 108 mm length, 44–47 mm width and 17–18 mm thickness. One user-guide edition instead prints 101 mm; that conflicting line is not treated as a new physical variant. Battery follows the user guide's 750 mAh figure.
- **CX75:** [Siemens user guide filed with the FCC](https://fcc.report/FCC-ID/PWX-CX75/550006.pdf), camera pages 48–50 and Product data page 139. Replaced 98 g / 112 × 48 × 19 mm with the guide's 102 g / 111.7 × 48 × 19.5 mm. The guide distinguishes still and preview/video resolutions; it does not establish VGA video recording, so that claim was removed. RS-MMC and Bluetooth are retained; unverified numeric zoom/Bluetooth-version/GPRS-class claims were dropped. Did not assert a record or technical first.
- **SXG75:** [manufacturer guide](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1564186/Siemens-Sxg75.html), [Product data](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/147990/siemens-sxg75.html?page=146), [service documentation](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/984224/Siemens-Sxg-75.html). Maintained 111 × 53 × 20 mm, 134 g, 1000 mAh and documented RS-MMC support up to 256 MB. Conflicting user-storage figures remain omitted. Reframed “the last Siemens” as a model from the closing Siemens Mobile period. GPS hardware is distinguished from software/services.
