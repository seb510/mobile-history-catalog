# W550i, Nokia 7280 and Nokia 6800

Three new records are translated into Ukrainian, Russian and English. Existing homepage lead models remain unchanged; the new records join the full editorial collections and include links to related models and comparisons.

## Sources and scope

- W550i (2005): [Sony Ericsson White Paper, July 2005](https://altehandys.de/downloads/wp-se-w550.pdf) and [manufacturer announcement, August 2005](https://www.webwire.com/ViewPressRel.asp?aId=3619). Screen, storage, camera, dimensions, weight, battery and networks follow the white paper. Its comparison columns describe W550 and K750i; the W550 column is used. No RAM or chipset values are inferred. The numeric weight is 120.1 g as specified by the manufacturer.
- Nokia 7280 (2004): [Nokia Fashion Collection announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2004/09/09/1846610/0/en/RED-BLACK-WHITE-DISTINCTLY-BOLD.html), [Nokia user guide](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/112210/Nokia-7280.html) and [technical overview on the service manual’s first page](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/847848/Nokia-7280.html). Spinner input, active slide, 104 × 208 display, VGA camera, BL-8N 700 mAh and radio/connectivity are documented. The user guide explicitly says its battery is internal and not user-removable. Unverified storage, physical dimensions and weight are omitted rather than filled from conflicting aggregators.
- Nokia 6800 (2003 market-release year; announced in 2002): [European NHL-6 user guide](https://i.smartphone.ua/docs/instr/instr_nokia-6800_eng.pdf), [Nokia US announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2003/08/04/1847021/0/en/AT-T-Wireless-Nokia-Announce-Immediate-Availability-of-the-Nokia-6800-Messaging-Phone.html), [Nokia technical information reproduced with its user guide](https://manualzz.com/doc/4191324/nokia-6800-mobile-phone-user-guide). European GSM 900/1800 is kept separate from American 850/1900. The battery and weight are BLC-2, 1000 mAh and 122 g; uncertain memory and display measurements are omitted. Its keyboard has a dedicated `fold-out-keyboard` construction label in all three interface languages, not a clamshell or foldable-screen label.
